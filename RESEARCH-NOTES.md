# Design research notes

The Nightfall Resonance expansion was designed after a focused mechanics research pass on My Singing Monsters and on the user's references for late-game boss-theme intensity.

## My Singing Monsters systems studied

Official Big Blue Bubble material was used to understand the structure rather than to copy assets. The research focused on:

- Islands functioning as distinct monster ensembles with destination-specific music and progression.
- Breeding and incubation as a core collection loop.
- Castle bed capacity as a real roster constraint.
- Level progression and teleport/transfer requirements for special late-game islands.
- Seasonal monsters being limited to event windows and using special acquisition/breeding rules.
- Wubbox-style powering through boxing required monsters rather than ordinary purchasing alone.
- Rare Wubbox requiring boxed Rare monsters.
- Gold Island Epic Wubbox changing visually and musically as it gains powered phases.
- Late-game island concepts where source monsters are transformed or transferred into a new destination rather than simply purchased again.
- The importance of monsters having visible performance behavior connected to their musical role.

Official references reviewed include Big Blue Bubble's My Singing Monsters pages and update posts, including Update 2.4.1, Update 3.1, Update 3.3, and Update 4.0.0.

## Boss-theme reference research

The user cited Tower Battles Battlefront / Tower Battles-style themes and bosses including Void2, Lord Boss4, Void, Frosty, and SantaBot as the desired emotional intensity for the hardest Islet. Research confirmed the final-boss framing of Void2 and Lord Boss4 and the historical final-boss role of SantaBot. No external soundtrack was downloaded, sampled, transcribed, or recreated.

Instead, Null Crown Islet translates the requested qualities into an original system: 150 BPM, Phrygian harmony, dark organ, taiko-like impact percussion, distorted lead, cinematic brass, ritual choir, phrase escalation, and a final Primordial layer.

## Originality boundary

This repository includes no Big Blue Bubble or Tower Battles art, audio, monster/island names, code, models, sprites, or extracted assets. Creature SVGs, rarity morphs, island scenery, UI, event names, progression tables, synthesized parts, and musical sequences are original to Melodic Isles.
